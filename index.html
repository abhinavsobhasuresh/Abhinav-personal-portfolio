I designed a custom development board centered around an STM32F103C8T6 microcontroller, featuring a compact four-layer stackup to ensure stable signal integrity for the high-speed crystal oscillator. The project integrated a dedicated USB-C interface for power and data, along with an onboard SWD header to streamline firmware debugging and peripheral testing.

I engineered a high-performance hardware solution utilizing the ESP32-S3 SoC, specifically optimizing the RF layout and power delivery network to support reliable Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE connectivity.
